How we work

No product to sell —
but we work like FDEs

A Forward Deployed Engineer goes on site and solves the customer's problem hands-on. Because HITZET has no product to push, we pick the solution that fits your environment and goals rather than a particular tool.

Our goal is landing, not delivery. We don't stop at handing over a model or a report. We stay until the result is actually used and measured inside your systems and workflows — and step back once your team can run it without us.

STEP 1 · EMBED

Embed

Meet the people, data, systems and rules first-hand. We work only inside environments you approve.

STEP 2 · FRAME

Frame

Turn "we want less fraud" into numbers and constraints: losses, false-positive cost, analyst capacity.

STEP 3 · BUILD

Build

Start from the simplest baseline, then iterate on models, rules and optimization in short cycles.

STEP 4 · LAND

Land

Connect to production and set up performance monitoring and exception handling with your team.

STEP 5 · HAND OVER

Hand over

Transfer code, documentation, decision records and training — call us back only when you need to.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is ORFE, and why does it matter?

ORFE stands for Operations Research and Financial Engineering. Because it combines optimization, probability models and statistical learning, we can solve not only "how much fraud can we catch?" but also "with limited analysts, which cases should we look at first?" — in one coherent approach.

Do you sell a software product?

No. That's why we never push a particular product. We build the models, rules and tools you need on top of the systems and open-source software you already use, and ownership and operation stay with you.

How do you handle data security?

We work only inside environments you approve — your internal network or approved cloud. Personal data is kept to a minimum and pseudonymized, and we follow your security policies and agreements.

Can we start small?

Yes. We recommend a short assessment or a single build sprint first, then expanding once the impact is confirmed in numbers.

Do you only work with financial institutions?

No. Payments, commerce, fintech and platforms — any service where money and transactions flow — can use the same fraud detection and risk modeling methods.

Do you work with clients outside Korea?

Yes. We are based in Korea and work in Korean and English. Remote engagements are possible; on-site work depends on scope and location.
